Who we are

OilRise Petroleum Services is an Egyptian supply company founded in 2025 and based in Cairo. We supply OCTG, sucker rod lift equipment and wellheads to oil and gas operators and service companies working in Egypt.

We started OilRise with a simple aim: to give operators a supplier that is fast, transparent and easy to work with, backed by a reliable network of international manufacturers.

The people behind OilRise

OilRise was founded in 2025, but the people behind it are not new to the industry.

Before OilRise, our team spent 16 years supplying oilfield and petroleum equipment, from tubulars and wellhead components to production equipment.

From requirement to delivery

  1. Step 1

    Requirement

    You send us your material list, specification or tender package.

  2. Step 2

    Sourcing

    We match each item to a qualified manufacturer and confirm grade, specification and lead time.

  3. Step 3

    Quotation

    You receive a clear, itemized offer with delivery terms.

  4. Step 4

    Inspection

    Material is checked against the order specification before shipment.

  5. Step 5

    Delivery

    We manage shipping and customs clearance through to your site or warehouse.
